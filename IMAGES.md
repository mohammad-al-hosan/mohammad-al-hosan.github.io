## dg-mosfet-short-channel  (assets/img/dg-mosfet-short-channel/)
- fig1-structure.png: Device structures - The four simulated variants: UO-DG (uniform oxide), SO-DG (step oxide), DMSO-DG (step oxide + dual-material gate) and Halo-DMSO-DG (adds junction halo).
- fig2-uo-transfer.png: Stage 1: transfer characteristics - I<sub>D</sub>–V<sub>GS</sub> at V<sub>DS</sub> = 0.8 V for uniform oxide from 0.8 to 2.0 nm. Thicker oxide raises I<sub>OFF</sub> and lowers I<sub>ON</sub>.
- fig3-uo-bands.png: Stage 1: band profiles - OFF-state conduction and valence band edges along the channel. Thicker oxide gives stronger drain-side band bending and a lower source-side barrier.
- fig4-uo-trends.png: Stage 1: DIBL, SS and f<sub>T</sub> - DIBL and SS rise with oxide thickness (left); f<sub>T</sub> also rises with oxide thickness (right), so thinning the oxide costs speed.
- fig5-so-current-density.png: Stage 2: OFF-state current density - Atlas total current density at V<sub>GS</sub> = 0, V<sub>DS</sub> = 0.8 V for the uniform-oxide and step-oxide devices.
- fig6-so-potential.png: Stage 2: potential perturbation - Δψ(x) along the channel between V<sub>DS</sub> = 0 and 0.8 V. The drain-induced change stays more confined to the drain side in the step-oxide device.
- fig7-so-bands.png: Stage 2: band profiles - Conduction and valence bands at V<sub>DS</sub> = 0.08 V and 0.8 V. The shift near the source-side barrier is smaller for SO-DG, consistent with its lower DIBL.
- fig8-dmg-bands.png: Stage 3: conduction band vs ΔΦ - Conduction band along the gate in the OFF state (left) and ON state (right). The gate-material boundary creates a potential step that raises the source-side barrier as ΔΦ increases.
- fig9-dmg-current.png: Stage 3: current density - ON-state (left axis) and OFF-state (right axis) current density for ΔΦ from 0.1 to 0.3 eV. OFF-state current falls as ΔΦ rises.
- fig10-dmg-ft.png: Stage 3: f<sub>T</sub> vs V<sub>GS</sub> - The f<sub>T</sub> peak moves to higher V<sub>GS</sub> as ΔΦ increases, following the rise in V<sub>th</sub>.
- fig11-dmg-potential.png: Stage 3: potential maps - Atlas potential distribution at V<sub>GS</sub> = V<sub>DS</sub> = 0.8 V for ΔΦ = 0.1, 0.2 and 0.3 eV.
- fig12-halo-schematic.png: Stage 4: halo concept - Depletion-region extent without and with P<sup>+</sup> halo. The higher local doping keeps the drain-side depletion boundary closer to the junction.
- fig13-halo-trends.png: Stage 4: halo concentration sweep - DIBL and SS (left), I<sub>ON</sub>/I<sub>OFF</sub> (middle) and f<sub>T</sub> (right) versus halo concentration. DIBL and leakage improve with diminishing returns, while f<sub>T</sub> falls at the highest level.
