Sequential Mitigation of Short-Channel Effects in 20 nm DG-MOSFETs

An electrostatic engineering approach using Step-Oxide, Dual-Material Gate and Halo doping

The continued scaling of Double-Gate (DG) MOSFETs is constrained by short-channel effects (SCEs), above all Drain-Induced Barrier Lowering (DIBL). This study applies a sequential structural methodology to decouple source and drain electrostatics. By adding an asymmetric Step-Oxide (SO), a Dual-Material Gate (DMG) and localized Halo doping, DIBL falls from a baseline of 67.21 mV/V to below 30 mV/V, while ON-state drive and RF cutoff frequency are tracked at every step.
