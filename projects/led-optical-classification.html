Optical Quality Classification of LEDs Using KNN and Random Forest

A low-cost optical characterization rig and a machine-learning pipeline that classifies LED quality from measured optical features.

2026Year
BPW34 photodiode, TCS34725 sensor, ArduinoHardware
KNN, Random ForestModels
ICECE 2026 paper, under reviewOutcome

Motivation

  • Visual or spectrometer-based LED screening is slow or expensive.
  • A cheap sensor setup plus a classifier could grade LEDs consistently.

How it was done

  1. Measurement rig

    • BPW34 photodiode and TCS34725 colour sensor read by an Arduino.
    • Readings streamed to the PC over serial (PySerial).

  2. Feature engineering

    • Features derived from the raw optical readings before training.

  3. Classification

    • KNN and Random Forest models trained and compared (scikit-learn, pandas).

  4. Interface

    • Streamlit app for live measurement and classification.
