IoT-Based Monitoring and Preservation System for Small-Scale Fruit Storage

An embedded system that senses storage conditions, actuates cooling and misting automatically, logs to the cloud, and allows a remote override.

2025Year
Arduino Mega + ESP8266Controller
DHT22, MQ135Sensors
ICECTE 2026, IEEE XplorePublished

Motivation

  • Small-scale storage loses fruit to heat, humidity, and ripening gases, and cold-chain equipment is out of reach for small operators.

How it was done

  1. Sense

    • DHT22 for temperature and humidity; MQ135 for air quality.

  2. Act

    • Relay-driven Peltier cooling and misting.

  3. Connect

    • ESP8266 for cloud logging and remote override.
