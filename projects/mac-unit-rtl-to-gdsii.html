Academic project · Level 4, Term 2

RTL-to-GDSII Implementation of an Optimised Multiply-Accumulate Unit

A parameterized MAC unit taken from RTL to GDSII on two independent flows, one commercial and one open-source, to check whether synthesis conclusions hold across toolchains.

2026Year
Cadence Genus/Innovus, GPDK045 (45 nm)Flow A
OpenROAD, IHP SG13G2 (130 nm)Flow B
ICECE 2026 paper, under reviewOutcome

Motivation

  • The MAC is the core of DSP and ML accelerators; its area, timing, and power depend heavily on synthesis constraints.
  • Most results come from a single toolchain. Repeating the study on a second flow tests whether the trends are real or tool artifacts.

How it was done

  1. Parameterized RTL

    • SystemVerilog MAC unit with configurable parameters.

  2. Synthesis constraint sweep

    • Constraints varied and the resulting area, timing, and power compared.

  3. Physical implementation

    • Place and route to GDSII in Innovus on GPDK045 and in OpenROAD on IHP SG13G2.

  4. Cross-toolchain validation

    • Same design and sweeps on both flows to compare trends.
