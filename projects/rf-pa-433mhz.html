Class-A RF Power Amplifier for the 433 MHz ISM Band

A common-emitter Class-A power amplifier designed and simulated in Cadence Virtuoso on a 90 nm process.

2025Year
gpdk090 (90 nm)Process
433 MHz ISMBand
ICECTE 2026, IEEE XplorePublished

Motivation

  • 433 MHz ISM links need an amplifier with enough gain and output power at low complexity, and a good input match.

How it was done

  1. Design

    • Class-A common-emitter stage biased for linear operation.

  2. Simulate

    • Gain, saturated output power, power-added efficiency, and S-parameters in Cadence Virtuoso; QUCS Studio used alongside.

Figures

Results

  • 10.11 dB gain
  • 18.01 dBm Psat
  • 7.52% power-added efficiency
  • S11 of about −33.5 dB
