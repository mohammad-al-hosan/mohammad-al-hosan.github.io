Schottky-to-Ohmic Contact Modulation in Janus MoSeTe/Graphene van der Waals Heterostructure

Perpendicular electric field and biaxial strain, a first-principles study

Systematic multi-pathway Ohmic contact engineering from a single van der Waals heterostructure has not been shown. Using DFT (PBE with D3 dispersion, Quantum ESPRESSO), this study follows the Se-face Janus MoSeTe/graphene interface from an n-type Schottky contact (Φn = 0.477 eV, Φp = 0.850 eV) to Ohmic contact by two mechanistically different routes. A perpendicular field acts almost as a rigid Fermi-level shift, while biaxial strain renormalizes the MoSeTe bandgap.

The Te-face interface is already close to p-type Ohmic at zero strain (Φp = 0.0185 eV) and becomes fully p-type Ohmic at +2 % strain. The Se face is the one with a sizeable barrier for both carriers, so it is the interface tuned in detail.

  • Equilibrium contact (Se face)n-type Schottky, Φn 0.477 eV
  • Te-face contactΦp 0.0185 eV, p-Ohmic at +2 %
  • Field to Ohmic (n / p)+0.6 / −0.20 V/Å
  • Strain to p-type Ohmic (Se face)switches at +4 to +6 %
  • Stability (Se face)no imaginary phonons; 300 K AIMD
