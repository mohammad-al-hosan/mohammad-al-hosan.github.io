Schottky-to-Ohmic Contact Modulation in Graphene/Janus MoSeTe van der Waals Heterostructures

A first-principles study of how a perpendicular electric field and biaxial strain tune the Schottky barrier at a graphene/Janus MoSeTe contact, and how the barrier can be driven to a fully Ohmic contact.

Apr 2025 – Jun 2026Period
Prof. S. M. Mominuzzaman, BUETSupervisor
DFT, Quantum ESPRESSOMethod
Journal extension under reviewStatus

Motivation

  • Contact resistance at the metal–semiconductor interface limits 2D field-effect transistors; a Schottky barrier throttles current injection.
  • Janus MoSeTe has an intrinsic out-of-plane dipole, so the Se side and the Te side of the same material can behave differently as a contact.
  • Goal: find controllable knobs that move the contact from Schottky to Ohmic without changing the materials.

How it was done

  1. Build the stacks

    • Graphene on Janus MoSeTe, Se-side and Te-side interfaces, with six stacking configurations considered.
    • Vacuum spacing along the stacking direction to isolate periodic images.

  2. Relax and compare stackings

    • GGA-PBE with PAW pseudopotentials and DFT-D3 van der Waals correction.
    • BFGS structural relaxation; binding energy used to rank stacking configurations.

  3. Electronic structure

    • SCF, then band structure along Γ–M–K–Γ, then projected wavefunctions (PDOS).
    • Fatband analysis to project bands onto each layer and read off the Schottky barrier height from the semiconductor band edges relative to the graphene Dirac point.

  4. Tune the contact

    • Perpendicular electric field sweeps (sawtooth field with dipole correction).
    • Biaxial strain sweeps over the heterostructure lattice.
    • Barrier height tracked as a function of each knob, separately and together.

  5. Explain the physics

    • Charge density difference and Bader charge analysis for interfacial charge transfer.
    • Work function, electron localization function, and planar-averaged electrostatic potential to link the barrier to the interface dipole.

  6. Device concept

    • Used the tunable contact behavior to propose a 2D field-effect transistor architecture.

Figures

Results

  • Four independent pathways to a fully Ohmic Graphene/MoSeTe contact, using electric field and biaxial strain to modulate the Schottky barrier.
  • A proposed 2D FET architecture that exploits the tunable contact.
